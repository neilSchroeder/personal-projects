\chapter{Summary}
\label{chap:summary}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

A measurement of the Higgs boson mass $m_\mathrm{H}$ was performed in the diphoton decay channel using 138 fb$^{-1}$ collected at a center-of-mass energy of 13 TeV. The precision of the measurement is now 
limited by the systematic uncertainties associated with the energy of photons measured by the ECAL. However, after combination with the H$\rightarrow$ZZ$\rightarrow$4$\ell$ measurement, this will be the most precise measurement of the Higgs boson mass until it is measured by dedicated future experiments.

This thesis presented my work on the following:
\begin{itemize}
    \item improving the accuracy of the measurement of the energy scale and resolution of the ECAL using electrons from Z boson decays,
    \item optimizing the diphoton MVA boundaries to improve the signal strength and relative resolution of the mass measurement,
    \item introducing new methods of determining systematic uncertainties on the electron residual energy scale and additional smearing corrections,
    \item and introducing new methods of determining the systematic uncertainty associated with the non-linearity between energy distributions of Z$\rightarrow$ee and H$\rightarrow\gamma\gamma$ events. 
\end{itemize}

My colleague Alexander Ledovskoy developed the ELM framework, and worked with Badder Marzocchi to derive the FNUF corrections and their uncertainties. This work drastically reduced the systematic uncertainty on the Higgs mass measurement in the diphoton channel.

My colleagues Rajdeep Chatterjee, Badder Marzocchi, Fabio Iemmi, Junquan Tao, and Zhenxuan Zhang performed the Higgs boson mass measurement using an analysis optimized for the full Run 2 dataset.

The Higgs boson mass in the diphoton channel was measured to be 
\begin{equation*}
    m_{\mathrm{H}} = 125.574 \pm 0.088 (\mathrm{stat.}) \pm 0.121 (\mathrm{syst.}) \mathrm{\ GeV}.
\end{equation*}
